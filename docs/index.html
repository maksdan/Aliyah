Aliyah Yomi

A daily portion of the week's parasha, Sunday through Friday.

The week's Torah portion, divided across the six days before Shabbat: an aliyah or two each weekday, and the Haftarah on Friday. Every reading is shown in Hebrew with the ta'amim, alongside an English translation, with Targum Onkelos available on its own tab.

How the daily portion is chosen

The app works out the current parasha from the Hebrew calendar on your device — no network needed for that part — and then follows a fixed division of that parasha across Sunday to Thursday, with the Haftarah on Friday. Shabbat itself has no reading. Combined parashiot (Matot-Masei, Vayakhel-Pekudei and the rest) are handled as a single week.

Ashkenazi and Sephardi

Where the two rites read a different Haftarah, a toggle appears on Friday. Your choice is remembered. On the weeks where both rites read the same Haftarah, no toggle is shown.

Reading a verse

  • Swipe a verse right to copy it, with its source reference.
  • Swipe a verse left to open it on Sefaria.
  • Tap a highlighted word in the English for a short definition.
  • Scroll to the end of the day's reading and it marks itself as read. You can also use the button at the bottom, which is how you un-mark a day.

Where the texts come from

The Hebrew, the English translation and Targum Onkelos are all fetched from Sefaria, the free library of Jewish texts. An internet connection is needed to load each day's reading.

Notifications

The app can remind you about the day's reading. The reminders are scheduled on your own device — nothing is sent to a server — and they stop for the day once you have read. You can turn them off at any time in the Settings app.
